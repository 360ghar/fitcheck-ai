Compiled from docs/product-specs/overview.md, core-features-notes.md, PRODUCT_SENSE.md.

## The problem

- Wardrobe blindness: people own more clothes than they realize; items get forgotten in the back of closets.
- Outfit decision fatigue: the daily struggle of choosing what to wear; time wasted on trial and error.
- Impulse shopping: buying items that don't match the existing wardrobe; duplicate purchases; money wasted on unworn clothing.
- No visualization: can't see how items look together before wearing them; no way to plan for occasions; no virtual try-before-buy.

## Personas

- Sarah, 28, marketing manager: 200+ clothing items, spends 15+ minutes daily choosing outfits, 3–4 impulse purchases per month, mobile-first, on social media daily.
- Minimalist Mike, 32: capsule wardrobe, tracks cost-per-wear.
- Priya, 24, influencer: shares outfit inspiration, optimizes content creation.
- Peter, 45, busy professional: needs weather-appropriate efficiency.

## The solution — FitCheck AI

A virtual closet with AI-powered outfit visualization.

### Core features (all shipped)

1. Wardrobe management — upload photos; AI batch-extracts items with attributes and images (parallel extraction, generation overlaps; SSE-backed real progress; review before anything is saved).
2. Outfit builder + visualization — visual mix-and-match; AI-generated outfit looks; realistic try-on visualization.
3. Smart analytics — usage tracking, cost-per-wear, underutilized items, wardrobe optimization.
4. Planning & calendar — calendar-based outfit planning, weather integration, occasion-based filtering.
5. Recommendations — wardrobe gaps, style suggestions, similar items, weather suggestions, shopping recommendations.
6. Shopping intelligence — gap analysis, virtual try-before-buy, sustainability scoring.
7. Social — share outfits (public shared pages), community + stylists.
8. Gamification — streaks, achievements, leaderboard.

### Product principles

- Photo-first, low typing — extraction and generation reduce manual cataloging.
- Review before commit — AI proposals are drafts; users confirm writes.
- Honest AI limits — recoverable failures and clear status over silent wrongness.
- Privacy-aware defaults — user-owned wardrobe data; share flows are explicit.
