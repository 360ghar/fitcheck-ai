MOTION DIRECTOR — START HERE

Turn any idea into a finished 10–20 second video, with original music, in about 10 minutes.

You need:
  - Claude Code (https://claude.com/claude-code). It comes with a paid Claude plan.
  - Node.js 18 or newer (https://nodejs.org). If you don't have it, Claude will tell you.
  - A Mac. (Windows and Linux should work, but haven't been tested yet.)

Install (about 2 minutes, one time):
  1. Unzip this folder.
  2. Open a terminal in this folder and type:  claude
  3. Type:  hi
     Claude installs Motion Director and checks it works.

Make a video, anytime, in any Claude Code session:
  Type  /motion-director  (or just ask: "make me a video about ...")
  Answer a couple of quick questions. Your MP4 appears in the "Motion Director Videos" folder on your Desktop.
