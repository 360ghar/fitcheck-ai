# FitCheck AI icon concepts — generation prompts

Provider: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`. One separate generation per concept. Generated images are selection previews; production assets are not changed.

## 01 — Silk Check

File: `01-silk-check.png`

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: original FitCheck AI app-icon concept for an AI wardrobe and personal styling product.
Primary request: create one exceptionally polished, distinctive icon with the precision of a professionally art-directed technology brand.
Output/composition: square 1024 x 1024 image. One isolated symbol, centered optically, front-facing with orthographic geometry. The symbol occupies approximately 62% of the canvas width and no more than 66% of its height, with generous clear margins. The background fills the entire square edge to edge; it is a calm uniform field. The canvas itself is the app icon, not a picture of an icon sitting on a background. No extra rounded-square tile or border.
Quality: exact smooth contours, balanced proportions, strong figure-ground contrast, readable visual weight, simple large negative spaces. Any requested depth must enhance the silhouette and use studio-quality controlled lighting. The form must remain clear as a one-color silhouette and be feasible to reconstruct later as vector paths.
Exclusions: no wordmark, no tagline, no captions, no numbers, no watermark, no decorative stars or sparkles, no particles, no mockup, no device, no floor, no pedestal, no surrounding objects, no thin hairlines, no noisy textures, no complex reflections, no copied existing brand symbol. Letters are permitted ONLY where the concept explicitly requests a monogram or letterform.

Concept 01: Silk Check.
Design specification: A single broad pearl-white satin ribbon folds ONCE into an original asymmetrical check-shaped silhouette. A short lower-left arm flows through a compact, gently rounded valley into a longer upper-right arm. The one visible turnover exposes a cool pale-blue underside and creates a crisp diagonal crease. The ribbon feels cut and tailored, with softly curved edges and broad clean faces, not crumpled fabric. Its signature is the sculpted turn at the valley. White #F3F4F0 with a cool blue underside on a deep saturated cobalt #123DC9 field. Satin finish, broad soft highlights, subtle occlusion only at the fold; highly legible shape.
Deliver only this single finished icon image.
```
